Privacy Policy

LangTool · Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who is responsible

LangTool is a personal portfolio project developed and operated by Loránd Dallos. Questions or requests about your data can be sent through the contact form on the portfolio site.

What data is collected

  • Account data: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the name and profile details Google shares with the app (name, email address, profile picture link).
  • Your dictionary: the language packs and entries you create (words, grammar forms, translations, examples, tags, notes) and when they were created or changed.
  • Technical data: the hosting and backend providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and operation.

How it is used

Data is used only to sign you in and to store and synchronise your dictionary between your devices. LangTool has no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

Where it is stored

  • On your device: a local copy of your dictionary and your sign-in session are kept in the app's storage (in the browser's storage for the web version).
  • Supabase: authentication and the cloud copy of your dictionary, hosted in the EU (Ireland). Access is restricted so that each account can only read and change its own data.
  • Firebase Hosting (Google): serves the web app's files.
  • Google: only if you choose "Continue with Google", to verify your identity.

Cookies

LangTool does not use tracking cookies. It uses the browser's local storage only to keep you signed in and to store your dictionary on your device.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept while your account exists. Items you delete in the app are marked as deleted so the deletion can reach your other devices, and are removed together with your account. You can ask for your account and all its data to be deleted at any time through the contact form above.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to its processing, through the contact form. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
