LangTool

A German vocabulary dictionary that works offline and syncs across devices.

What it does

LangTool lets you build your own dictionaries ("language packs") while learning German. Each entry stores the word with its grammar (e.g. article and plural for nouns, Präteritum and Perfekt for verbs), translations, example sentences, tags and notes. Entries are shown as a timeline grouped by the day they were added.

  • Search by German word or translation, ignoring accents, with typo-tolerant fallback results.
  • Duplicate protection: each word and word type exists only once per pack, and likely typos of existing words are flagged before saving.
  • Sign in with email or Google; your dictionary is backed up and kept in sync between devices.
  • Works offline: everything is stored on the device first and synced when a connection is available.

How it is built

Flutter (Dart) with an MVVM architecture, a local SQLite database (Drift, also in the browser via WebAssembly), and Supabase for authentication and a Postgres backend protected by row-level security. The web version is deployed to Firebase Hosting by a GitHub Actions pipeline that runs the test suite on every change.
